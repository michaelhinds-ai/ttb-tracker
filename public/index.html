Maintenance & one-time fixes

New bottlings and orders handle these automatically. Use them once to fix up older records, or after restoring a backup. All are safe to re-run and can be undone from Recent Activity on the dashboard.

Reconcile past dumps — gives every past bottling a searchable "dumped" barrel record on the Barrels page. Tracking only; doesn't change report totals.
Recalculate dump losses — recomputes angel's-share loss on older bottlings from the source barrel's proof gallons.
Backfill gift-shop orders — creates a $0 order trail (and links the GiftShop customer) for past gift-shop bottlings.
Route bottlings through Processing — records the bulk transfer into the Processing account (and its angel’s-share loss) for every past bottling, so Processing and Bulk on Hand read correctly. Does not change any tax figure.
Recompute Processing from bottled amounts — rebuilds each bottling’s Processing entries from the bottles you actually counted (× proof) plus the bottling loss % above, instead of the barrel’s estimate. This removes impossible “gains” and negative on-hand, and applies going forward too. It changes past months’ Processing figures, so re-file any month you’ve already submitted.

What this covers

Federal (TTB): Production (5110.40), Storage (5110.11), Processing (5110.28) monthly reports, and the quarterly Excise Tax Return (5000.24) with CBMA tiered rates.
Kentucky: Monthly distilled spirits return — excise ($1.92/gal), 11% wholesale sales tax, and $0.05/case case tax — plus the annual barrel ad valorem tax with the HB 5 phase-out (96% of tax in 2026, declining to full exemption in 2043).
This tool is a record-keeping and calculation aid reflecting federal CBMA rates and Kentucky distilled-spirits taxes as researched for 2026. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the TTB or the Kentucky Department of Revenue and does not file on your behalf. Rates and rules change — always reconcile against the official forms and confirm current figures at ttb.gov and revenue.ky.gov before filing. The barrel tax is a local property tax; rates vary by county/district. Consult your compliance advisor for your permit and locality.
