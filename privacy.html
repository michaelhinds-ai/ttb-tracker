Privacy Policy

TTB Reporting Tracker, operated by Louisville Barrel Company LLC. Last updated August 12, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how the TTB Reporting Tracker application ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. The App is a private, internal record-keeping and reporting tool used by Louisville Barrel Company LLC to track distilled spirits production, storage, processing, barrel inventory, finished goods, orders, and federal (TTB) and state tax reporting, and to create sales invoices in connected accounting software.

Information We Handle

The App stores the operational data you enter — such as production and removal records, barrel and finished-goods inventory, customers, and orders. When you connect QuickBooks Online, the App accesses and creates accounting records (customers, items, and invoices) in the QuickBooks company you authorize, using tokens provided by Intuit's OAuth service. The App does not collect personal information from the general public and is not offered as a public service.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to operate the App for Louisville Barrel Company LLC — to produce compliance reports, manage inventory and orders, and synchronize sales invoices with connected accounting software. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing, and we do not use it for advertising.

QuickBooks / Intuit Data

If you connect QuickBooks Online, the App receives OAuth access and refresh tokens from Intuit and stores them securely to create and read accounting records on your behalf. This access is limited to the QuickBooks company you authorize and is used only to perform the actions you initiate in the App. You may disconnect QuickBooks at any time, which revokes the App's access and deletes the stored tokens.

Data Storage & Security

Application data and connection tokens are stored using the App's hosting provider (Netlify) and its managed storage. Access to a workspace requires a private link/code, and connection secrets are kept in server-side environment variables, not exposed to browsers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information the App handles.

Data Retention

Operational data is retained for as long as it is needed for business record-keeping and tax compliance. QuickBooks tokens are retained until you disconnect. You may export or erase your workspace data from within the App.

Your Choices

You control what data is entered into the App, you can export a full backup at any time, and you can disconnect connected services or erase your workspace.
